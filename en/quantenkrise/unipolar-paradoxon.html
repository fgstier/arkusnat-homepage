03. The Weber Approach (1846) – A Traitor in the CM System

The approach by Wilhelm Eduard Weber (1846) is a historical alternative to Maxwell's equations. Weber proved that magnetism does not require an abstract field, but is the direct result of velocity- and acceleration-dependent central forces between charges.

CM MAXWELL LOGIC

Magnetism is an entangled force (Lorentz) acting perpendicular to motion and field.

WEBER MODEL (1846)

Magnetism is the direct result of the relative velocity of charges. Causality remains central.

NPI POSTULATE

Magnetism is the timeless, static order in the NPI grid. No field rotation required.

// WHY THE CM SUPPRESSED WEBER:
  • Instantaneous Interaction: Weber's formula implies timeless action-at-a-distance. This shatters the CM axiom of light speed $c$.
  • No Fields: The absence of abstract fields undermines the foundation of CM field theory.
